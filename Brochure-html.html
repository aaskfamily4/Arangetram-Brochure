Welcome

🌸

An Arangetram is the first solo recital of a student of Bharatanatyam and marks the culmination of many years of dedicated training. The word Arangetram is derived from two Tamil words — arangam (stage) and etram (to ascend) — meaning “to ascend the stage.”

Bharatanatyam is one of the oldest Indian classical dance traditions, with roots tracing back to the 3rd century B.C. The term Bharatanatyam is a composite of bharata and natyam. Bharata is formed from bha (bhava — emotion), ra (raga — melody), and ta (tala — rhythm), while natyam means dance in Sanskrit.

Originally performed in the temples of Tamil Nadu in South India, Bharatanatyam integrates three essential elements: nritta, the pure dance characterized by rhythmic footwork and intricate movements; nritya, which combines rhythm with expressive storytelling; and natya, the dramatic portrayal of narratives through expressive gestures, graceful movement, and nuanced facial expressions.

An Arangetram does not signify the completion of training or the end of a dancer’s journey. Rather, it marks the student’s readiness to progress to deeper exploration and artistic maturity within this classical art form. It is the blossoming of the dancer into a confident and accomplished performer. Anannya participates in this centuries-old tradition of the Bharatanatyam Arangetram.

We thank you for being a part of this auspicious occasion and warmly invite you to enjoy the presentation.

Program

🌸

  • 1  · THODAYA MANGALAM

    A traditional invocation dance composition in Ragamalikai set to a garland of rhythms, thalamalika in praise of Lord Rama and Lord Krishna.

  • 2  · KANCHA DALAYA DAKSHI

    A beautiful devotional composition describing the radiant beauty and compassionate grace of the divine Mother in Kamalamanohari Ragam set to Adhi thalam.

  • 3  · VARNAM

    A traditional varnam depicting a love-lorn nayika longing for Lord Muruga. Her sakhi approaches the Lord as her messenger, appealing to his compassion and urging him to come to the maiden before her unbearable longing breaks her heart. The sakhi describes the nayika’s intense suffering and pleads with Muruga to hasten to her. A composition of Shri Papanasam Sivan in Sriranjani ragam set to Aadi thalam.

  • 4  · AADI KONDAAR

    A vibrant ode to Lord Nataraja, celebrating his majestic grace and bliss of Shiva’s cosmic dance through dynamic nritta and expressive abhinaya. A composition of Muthu Thandavar in Ragam: Mayamalavagowla set to Adi Thalam.

  • 5  · CHALIYE KUNJANAMO

    A graceful portrayal of Radha and Krishna, this composition captures the playful charm and tender emotions of their divine love. Through expressive abhinaya and lyrical movement, the dancer brings alive the beauty of Vrindavan and the enchanting presence of Krishna. A composition of Swati Tirunal in Ragam: Vrindavani set to Adi Thalam.

  • 6  · SNAKE DANCE

    A captivating Bharatanatyam composition inspired by the graceful, sinuous movements of the serpent. Through rhythmic footwork, expressive gestures, and fluid movements, the dancer brings alive the mystery, energy, and hypnotic beauty of the snake in Punnagavarali ragam set to Adhi thalam.

  • 7  · THILLANA

    Thillana is a traditional finale dance composition in Bharatanatyam, celebrated for its intricate footwork, rhythmic patterns, and sculpturesque poses. A vibrant celebration of Lord Krishna, this thillana captures his playful charm, grace, and divine beauty. Composition of the legendary composer, Shri Lalgudi Jayaraman in Madhuvanthi ragam set to Adhi thalam.

  • 8  · NINRANDA MAYILONDRU

    A vivid celebration of Krishna in the enchanting landscape of Brindavan, where peacocks dance, bees hum, and Krishna fills the air with the melody of his flute. Through graceful nritta and expressive abhinaya, the composition journeys from the beauty of nature to an inward discovery of the divine Brindavan within, culminating in the joyful invocation of Govinda and Radhe. A composition of Oothukadu Venkata Kavi.

  • 9  · MANGALAM

    A brief finale composition, whereby the dancer thanks God, her Guru and the audience for the completion of this recital.

Narthaki

Anannya Karthikeyan

Anannya is a sophomore at Emerald High School and has been learning Bharatanatyam for the past 8 years. She started her tutelage under the guidance of Guru Smt. Sweta Dixit and currently she has been learning under the able guidance of Guru Smt. Meenakshi Ganesan for the past 2 years. She is also learning Carnatic music for the past 5 years.

Anannya has been a dance lover since the age of 5. In class, she is a quiet observer, enthusiastic learner, and has been an accountable teaching assistant to younger kids in her dance school. Apart from dance, Anannya loves singing, reading books, and spending time with family.

She has participated and won accolades in competitions such as OSAAT, Papanasam Sivan, Art of India, and Veenadhari. Anannya is currently doing her diploma in Bharatanatyam under Aria University to deepen her knowledge of dance. She also completed the Junior Certification course in Bharatanatyam in SAMPADA.

The Orchestra

A Bharatanatyam performance is never a solo — it is a temple ritual conducted together. We are deeply honored to present these distinguished artists.

Guru Meenakshi Ganesan

Guru Meenakshi Ganesan

Guru Meenakshi Ganesan is the Founder and Artistic Director of the Kalaanjali School of Dance (Kalaanjali Dance Company), established in 2003. She began her Bharatanatyam training at the age of six under her aunt Guru Prema Nagasundaram and Guru Padmini Radhakrishnan, and holds the Visharad degree and a Master’s degree in Bharatanatyam. She is a recent graduate from her second Master’s degree in Dance from Aria University, and is pursuing her PhD in Dance from the same, continuing her scholarly engagement with the art form.

With over 3,000 performances to her credit across India, the UK, Canada, and the United States, Guru Meenakshi is an accomplished performer and choreographer. Her contributions to Indian classical dance have earned her several honors, including the prestigious title “Nritya Mayuri” (Dancing Peacock) conferred by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. She has collaborated extensively with eminent musicians and dancers and has led numerous thematic and ensemble productions across the U.S.

A dedicated teacher and mentor, Guru Meenakshi has successfully guided over 50 Arangetrams through Kalaanjali. Her students have received accolades at renowned platforms such as the Cleveland Thyagaraja Aradhana competitions, FETNA, the prestigious National Young Arts (The National Foundation for the Advancement of Artists). She currently trains students in Madison, Wisconsin, and the San Francisco Bay Area, as well as in India and Canada, through both in-person and online instruction.

She was recognized as a Master Artist by the Wisconsin Arts Board from 2015 to 2021, later serving on its board in 2022. She is the first recipient of the Tiruvarur Nagarajan Award (2024) from Chennai, Silicon Andhra award May 2026, and continues to contribute to the growth of Bharatanatyam through teaching, choreography, and arts advocacy. Based in Dublin, California, Guru Meenakshi remains deeply committed to nurturing the next generation of dancers.

Mridangam

Srinath Bala

Srinath Bala is a versatile percussionist with varied styles blended rhythmically into one. Having been initiated by Late Sri. Krishnan (Palghat Sri Mani Iyer style), he received intensive guidance under Sri. R. Ramesh (Karaikudi Mani Style) in Chennai, and later under Maestro Umayalpuram K. Sivaraman. To his credit, he has uniquely combined the kannakku and naadam aspects from various gurus.

He has accompanied the topmost senior artists performing in India, Canada, Zambia, Singapore, and the US, and has also played in Jugalbandis and TalaVadya Concerts to showcase the solo percussion aspects. He has various recordings and has given lecture-demonstrations on LAYAM. In 2006, he presented a TalaVadya concert titled “Music Through Membranes”.

Being a recipient of the PG Arts Award, MSAC Artist Awards, and titles “Kala Shreshtha” and “Paramparaa Rakshak”, he has over 30 years of concert experience and is an extremely well-sought-after artist for music and dance concerts. He has been teaching students for many years and has successfully trained many students, who currently play Mridangam, Ghatam, and Kanjira actively in various music circles.

Vocalist

He has won several prizes in regional and national Carnatic vocal competitions in the USA including the title of Carnatic Star (MN) and many prizes at the Cleveland Thyagaraja Aradhana. Post his Arangetram in 2018, Surya has been a regular performer at the prestigious Chennai margazhi seasons in and the Cleveland Thyagaraja Aradhana. He was also awarded the Best Carnatic Vocalist award at the Global Music Competitions held by Spring Nectar Foundation. Surya has been a lead vocalist for several dance Arangetrams and recitals.

Apart from Carnatic Vocal, Surya is blessed to be under the tutelage of Kalaimamani Shri. Tiruvarur Vaidyanathan for Mridangam and regularly accompanies several artists in the prestigious Chennai margazhi season and the Cleveland Aradhana. Surya is a recent graduate of UC Davis and has been the Vice President and Road Captain of the UC Davis Cycling team and the treasurer of the UC Davis carnatic music team Dhvani.

Violin

Sandhya Srinath

Sandhya is an innately talented artist who showcases her true talent in every bow. A gold medalist in her master's program in music and an early A-grade awardee for the All India Radio, Sandhya has firmly established the school of Late Shri V. Janakiraman of AIR, Delhi. She learnt, and skillfully absorbed, the styles of maestros Late Shri Lalgudi G Jayaraman and Prof. T.N. Krishnan within her.

With over 40 years of performances, along with top solo performances worldwide, she has also accompanied many senior musicians and dancers. Some of her coveted awards include the Best Young Violinist award from the Music Academy, Chennai; the Individual Artist Award from both the PG Arts Council and the Maryland State Arts Council. She has also received titles such as Naada Brahma Kala Bhooshanam, Naada Brahma Kalai Mani, Vaadhya Kala Bhaarathi, Kala Shreshtha, and Paramparaa Rakshak.

She has numerous recordings to her credit and her versatility has led her to take on instrumental ensembles, jugalbandis with Hindustani artists, and fusion concerts with western musicians. Known for her sensitive renditions and scintillating artistry, she is a very highly sought-after artist. She also actively teaches Violin to students all over the US.
